                      About

                      GiveHeart connects people who cannot pay an essential bill (electricity, phone or water) with donors who want to help. Donors pay the bill directly to the biller, never cash to a person.
                      All names, bills, amounts and receipts are made up. No real money is taken or sent.
                      We are not a bank, a lender or a government office. We do not give legal, medical or financial advice.
                      Give true information. The bill must be yours or your household's. One open request per meter or account. The photos must be yours. We may call you. False information means removal and a block.
                      A donation is a free gift. It is not a loan or an investment and gives no profit or interest. We cannot promise that a bill will be paid or accepted. [Refund and fee rules will be added before real payments.]
                      A bill is "Verified" only after we check it. Verified does not promise payment.
                      Use "Report a problem". We answer within 2 days.

                      Community Guidelines

                      GiveHeart works because people trust each other. These rules protect donors and people who ask for help.

                      1. Be honest.
                      Give true information. Fake bills, edited photos or someone else's meter are not allowed.
                      2. Use your own bill.
                      Ask only for a bill that is yours or your household's. One open request for each meter or account.
                      3. Respect people.
                      No insults, threats, hate or shame. Do not share anyone's bill, photo or phone number outside GiveHeart without their clear OK.
                      4. No private deals.
                      Never ask a donor to send money, airtime or gifts outside the app. Do not put phone numbers in notes.
                      5. No spam.
                      No selling, advertising or recruiting.
                      6. Protect children.
                      Never show a child's face or school details.
                      7. Keep your account safe.
                      Never share your PIN or code. One account for each person.
                      8. Report problems.
                      Use "Report this bill" for a bill that looks wrong, and "Report a problem" for anything else. False reports to hurt someone are not allowed.

                      If you break these rules, we may remove your request, block your account, and share information with the authorities if the law requires. We tell you the reason. You can appeal within [14] days using "Report a problem".

                      Donation Terms

                      1. A free gift.
                      A donation is a gift. It is not a loan or an investment. It gives no profit or interest, and you cannot ask for it back from the person helped.
                      2. Paid to the biller.
                      Your money pays the bill (EEU, Ethio telecom or the water office). It never goes to a person's bank or wallet. For EEU prepaid, the electricity token goes to the person who asked for help.
                      3. You see the total first.
                      You see the full amount before you pay. The test limit is 2,000 birr for each request.
                      4. Monthly help is your choice.
                      You pick the months and the day. We remind you 2 days before each payment. You can pause or cancel any time. Cancel stops future payments, not payments already made.
                      5. Fees.
                      GiveHeart fee: [none in this test / to be set]. Payments by card from abroad have card fees and currency conversion. You see them before you pay.
                      6. Receipts.
                      After each payment you get a receipt with a number, the biller, the amount and the date.
                      7. Final payments.
                      When the biller accepts a payment, it cannot be taken back, because the bill is paid.
                      8. If a payment fails.
                      If a biller refuses a payment, we tell you. [Refund rule to be set before real payments.]
                      9. No guarantee.
                      "Verified" means we checked the papers we received. It does not promise that a bill is true or will be paid. If you think a bill was fake, report it.
                      10. Limits.
                      We may set limits, and delay or refuse a payment that looks unsafe.
                      11. Your taxes.
                      You follow the tax and money rules of your own country.
                      12. Changes.
                      We may change these terms. We tell you in the app and ask you to agree again for big changes.

                      This test version takes no real money.

                      GiveHeart

                      Safety tips

                      FOR EVERYONE
                      • We never ask for your PIN, code or password. Not in the app, not by phone, not by message.
                      • GiveHeart never tells you to pay a person. All payments go to a biller inside the app.
                      • Contact us only through Help and Support or our official pages. Do not trust links from strangers who say they are GiveHeart.
                      • Keep your PIN private. Do not use 1234 or your birth year. Log out on a shared phone.
                      FOR DONORS
                      • Pay only inside the app. Never send money, airtime or gifts to someone who writes to you in private.
                      • Look at the proof and the "Verified" mark. Report a bill that looks wrong.
                      • If you are new, start with a small amount.
                      • Check each receipt, and check your monthly payments in Profile.
                      • Enter card details only on the payment screen.
                      FOR PEOPLE WHO ASK FOR HELP
                      • Ask only for your own bill.
                      • Never give your PIN or card details to anyone.
                      • Do not put your phone number in notes. We contact you ourselves.
                      • If anyone asks you for money to "approve" your request, it is a scam. Report it.
                      • Share your photo only if you want to.
                      IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
                      Use "Report a problem". If you lost money to a scam, also tell the police [local number] and your bank or telebirr.
